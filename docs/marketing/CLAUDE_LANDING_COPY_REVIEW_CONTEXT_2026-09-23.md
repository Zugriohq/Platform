# Zugrio Landing Copy Review Context — For Claude

Date: 23 September 2026  
Purpose: bring an external reviewer up to speed before reviewing the current landing-page copy  
Scope: **copy and positioning only**. Do not review or redesign the current visual system in this pass.

---

## 1. Why this brief exists

The current Zugrio landing copy has moved materially from the original Brand Proposition v2.2 application-test wording.

That movement is deliberate.

The original brand proposition is **not being discarded**, and the five-part grammar — **Market, Method, Moment, Mandate, Memory** — still underpins the product and message architecture. What changed is the way that architecture is exposed to a trader on a public marketing page.

The main lesson from applying the frozen copy to the actual website was that the page began to sound like:

- an internal architecture document;
- a product manual;
- a governance specification;
- or a company explaining itself instead of selling a compelling product.

The founder's direction is now:

> **Keep the architecture. Market the benefit.**

The public page must make Zugrio immediately understandable, distinctive and desirable to a trader without requiring them to learn Zugrio's internal conceptual vocabulary first.

This is therefore an **application-layer evolution** of the frozen brand proposition, not a repudiation of it.

## 2. What remains frozen / strategically intact

The following have **not** been abandoned:

### Master thesis

> **The chart is not the market.**

This remains the company-level brand thesis.

It is no longer required to be the first large hero headline on every acquisition page. On the current landing page it continues to operate as the deeper brand idea and closing signature.

### Category descriptor

> **Market-aware trading intelligence.**

Still active.

### Five-part structural grammar

> **Market. Method. Moment. Mandate. Memory.**

Still active as the underlying product/message grammar.

It should **not** be treated as a required public five-box framework or forced into literal retail labels.

The frozen v2.2 document already states that this is primarily a structural grammar and should not be marketed as a "5M framework" unless recognition later justifies it.

### Core strategic distinctions

These remain non-negotiable:

- market identity matters;
- a user's strategy/rules matter;
- current conditions can change a trade;
- a signal is not the same as permission or execution;
- the original decision and what happened afterwards stay connected;
- market families are not interchangeable because their charts happen to look similar;
- account/risk/execution authority remain distinct;
- outcomes do not rewrite decision quality;
- public readiness must not overstate actual release status.

### Initial markets

> **FX · Gold · Synthetic Indices**

Still the initial public product scope.

### Non-custodial direction

The trader's capital stays with the broker.

### Current-status truth

The site must continue to state that Zugrio is a **private build**, capabilities are under validation, public trading access is not yet open, and no performance claim is being made.

## 3. Why the five Ms are no longer literal public labels

The five Ms still map directly into the page.

The difference is that the public language has been translated into language traders already use.

| Frozen grammar | Current trader-facing expression | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Market** | "Same setup. Different market. Different answer." | Strong, immediately understandable, still carries the original market-specific thesis. |
| **Method** | **Strategy** | "Method" is valid internally, but "strategy" is more natural to the general trading audience. |
| **Moment** | **Current conditions** / "A good setup can become a bad entry." | Explains the practical consequence rather than naming an abstract pillar. |
| **Mandate** | **Control** | "Mandate" is architecturally precise but sounds institutional/legal to many retail traders. "Control" communicates the user benefit immediately. |
| **Memory** | **Decision history / replay / behavioral analytics** | Traders understand review, history, replay and what they actually did more quickly than "Memory" as a conceptual pillar. |

The page should allow a trader to **feel the five-part logic without having to learn the five-part vocabulary**.

That is an important review principle.

Do not judge the current landing copy by asking:

> "Where is the word Method / Moment / Mandate / Memory?"

Instead ask:

> "Is each underlying job of Market / Method / Moment / Mandate / Memory still represented clearly and truthfully?"

The answer should remain yes.

## 4. Why the hero changed

### Frozen v2.2 hero direction

The frozen application-test subheadline was:

> Zugrio evaluates each opportunity in its market, against your method and current conditions, acts only within your mandate, and preserves the full decision.

This is architecturally accurate.

In application, however, it asks a first-time retail visitor to understand too much conceptual structure too early.

The current acquisition hero is now frozen as:

> **A good setup can become a bad entry.**

Supporting copy:

> **Zugrio finds setups that fit your strategy, evaluates each one in the market that produced it, and keeps rechecking what still qualifies — within your limits, with every decision on record.**

This is not intended to replace **The chart is not the market** as the master brand thesis.

It is the **marketing-page entry point**: what the trader gets, in plain language.

The deeper thesis remains present in the brand and page.

### Why this matters

The visitor should understand in seconds that Zugrio:

1. scans markets;
2. identifies developing setups;
3. follows their progression;
4. alerts the trader;
5. shows the reasoning and relevant levels;
6. helps determine what is appropriate next.

That is a much stronger five-second product comprehension result than beginning with internal architecture language.

## 5. Public-language shift: Method → Strategy

This is intentional.

"Method" remains valid in product architecture and source-of-truth documents.

On the public website, **strategy** is preferred because it is common trading language.

Current line:

> **Your strategy sets the rules. Zugrio keeps checking them.**

The product direction remains the same:

- Zugrio Core is the default starting strategy under validation;
- other clearly defined strategies can be supported;
- selecting a strategy must materially change how an opportunity is evaluated;
- one strategy is never assumed to be universally best;
- strategy and automation/control remain separate decisions.

Do not interpret the use of "strategy" as an architectural change from Method Profiles.

It is a translation layer.

---

## 6. Public-language shift: Mandate → Control

The frozen architecture is still authority/mandate based.

Public copy now generally says **control** because it is faster to understand.

Examples:

> **A signal is not the same as "trade now."**

> **Triggered does not mean trade now. Zugrio checks current price, costs, account risk and your control level before the next action.**

The underlying meaning remains:

- Signal;
- Semi-Auto;
- Auto;
- Full Auto;
- explicit user-defined limits;
- account/risk/strategy scope;
- no authority created simply because a commercial plan includes automation.

The architecture has not been weakened.

The retail wording has been simplified.

## 7. Opportunity-state change: WATCH → READY → FIRE became FORMING → READY → TRIGGERED

Earlier exploration used:

> WATCH → READY → FIRE

This was changed after review.

### Why WATCH was rejected

Zugrio's value includes removing the need for the trader to stare at charts continuously.

"Watch" can sound like an instruction to the trader rather than a state of the setup.

### Why FIRE was rejected as the formal state

"Fire" is energetic but risks sounding:

- gamified;
- like execution permission;
- like "take the trade now";
- or like the platform fired an order.

That conflicts with the core principle that a signal and execution authority are different.

### Current nomenclature

> **FORMING → READY → TRIGGERED**

Meaning:

- **FORMING** — a setup is developing;
- **READY** — required conditions are in place and the defined trigger is awaited;
- **TRIGGERED** — the trigger has occurred and the signal is created.

"Signal fired" may still be used naturally as a verb.

"FIRE" is no longer the formal public state.

### Current marketing expression

Headline:

> **See the setup before it becomes a signal.**

Body:

> **FORMING. READY. TRIGGERED. Zugrio marks what is confirmed, what is missing and what invalidates the setup — live on the chart.**

The detailed state definitions are intentionally carried by the visual progression rather than a long paragraph.

## 8. Major product propositions added or made more explicit

The current landing page now reflects product direction that was either absent or too implicit in the frozen brand block.

### A. Background scanning

Core trader problem:

> You cannot stare at every chart all day.

Current expression:

> **You don't need to stare at every chart all day.**

> Zugrio scans in the background and brings you back when a setup forms, triggers, changes or expires — with the reason and next step attached.

This turns the architecture into a tangible trader benefit.

### B. Live chart reasoning

This is a major differentiator and should remain visible.

Zugrio does not merely show a score after the fact.

The intended experience is that the chart shows what is:

- confirmed;
- still missing;
- awaited;
- invalidated;
- changed.

This is why the opportunity-progression section explicitly says the reasoning is marked **live on the chart**.

### C. Signal-age / current-entry recheck

A signal is a timestamped event.

The market may move before the trader acts.

Current headline:

> **The signal fired. The market kept moving.**

Current supporting line:

> Zugrio rechecks price, spread, account risk and your control level before the next step.

This is one of the strongest practical expressions of the original **Moment** pillar.

### D. Broker-state integrity

The system must not make order state look simpler than it is.

Current short proof line:

> **Prepared ≠ submitted ≠ filled ≠ protected.**

This compresses an important execution truth without turning the marketing page into an order-state specification.

### E. Behavioral Analytics

This is now treated as a marketable product capability, not buried inside "Memory."

Current headline:

> **Your P&L tells you what happened. Zugrio shows how you traded.**

Supporting copy:

> With read-only broker data, Zugrio spots patterns in how you trade — early entries, chased price, risk changes, early exits and overrides. System actions and your interventions stay separate.

Important boundary:

Zugrio does **not** claim a trade record proves that a trader felt fear, impatience, greed or another emotion.

Behavioral analytics should remain factual and event-based.

The deeper product logic separates:

1. decision quality;
2. execution quality;
3. financial outcome.

A losing trade may still reflect disciplined execution.  
A profitable trade may still contain a rule violation.

### F. Decision replay

The record is not just a journal entry after the outcome.

Replay is intended to show what was known **at the time**, without allowing later information to rewrite the earlier decision.

Passed, missed, expired and overridden cases belong in review too.

### G. Bring your own plan

Zugrio should remain useful to discretionary traders even when it does not automate their strategy.

Current section:

> **Bring your own plan.**

> Record the setup, invalidation and risk before you act. Zugrio keeps the plan beside the outcome, even when it does not automate the strategy.

This is an important expansion of the value proposition beyond "use our strategies."

### H. Readiness / capability truth

The original trust principle remains:

> **Know exactly what's live.**

The current marketing expression is deliberately simpler:

> **Readiness, without guesswork.**

> **Know what's live.**

The current website may show example status language, but it must never imply that an illustrative capability is actually released.

## 9. Why the copy was recently compressed

A previous revision correctly restored important product depth but then overcorrected.

The page began to explain:

- every state;
- every caveat;
- every mode;
- every evidence boundary;
- every workflow;
- every analytics nuance

inside the main marketing sections.

That was accurate but commercially weak.

The founder's latest direction is:

> This is a marketing page, not an essay.

Therefore the current hierarchy is:

### 1. Headline sells the advantage

Example:

> **Your P&L tells you what happened. Zugrio shows how you traded.**

### 2. One short paragraph proves it

Enough substance to make the claim credible.

### 3. The UI/visual carries detail

Examples:
- FORMING / READY / TRIGGERED;
- product preview;
- status rows;
- workflow steps.

### 4. FAQ carries secondary explanation

Examples:
- no-setup behavior;
- discretionary plans;
- replay;
- behavioral-analytics boundaries;
- mobile/desktop continuity;
- automation details.

### Review implication

**Do not reward longer copy simply because it is more complete.**

A homepage section is successful when it makes the value obvious and credible with the minimum explanation required.

If detail can move to:

- the UI;
- FAQ;
- product documentation;
- onboarding;
- help content;

it generally should.

## 13. Important truth boundaries for review

Claude should challenge the copy if it violates any of these.

### Do not imply guaranteed profitability

Never imply that:

- disciplined trading guarantees profit;
- behavioral analytics produces profit;
- Zugrio eliminates losses;
- a strategy is proven profitable without evidence;
- a win rate is known where it is not.

### Do not overclaim released capability

The page may describe the intended product in confident present tense, but current release truth is separately stated:

- private build;
- validation;
- no public trading access yet.

This distinction is deliberate:

> **Describe the product confidently. State release status honestly.**

### Do not imply custody

Funds remain in the user's broker account.

### Do not imply read-only broker access can trade

Behavioral analytics in Signal mode uses suitable read-only broker data where supported.

It does not grant execution permission.

### Do not infer emotion from behavior data

The system may identify:

- early entries;
- chased price;
- changed risk;
- early exits;
- overrides;
- repeated deviations.

It must not claim that those events prove fear, greed, impatience, revenge or another internal mental state.

### Do not collapse execution states

Prepared, submitted, acknowledged, filled and protected are not synonyms.

### Do not make the five Ms visible merely to prove the framework still exists

The framework is a structural grammar.

The public page should remain trader-first.

## 14. What Claude is being asked to review now

Please review the **current landing copy as a marketing/sales application of the frozen brand architecture**, not as a rewrite of the architecture itself.

Evaluate:

1. **Five-second comprehension**  
   Does a trader understand what Zugrio does quickly?

2. **Distinctiveness**  
   Are the strongest differences obvious?
   - market-specific intelligence;
   - setup progression;
   - live reasoning;
   - current-entry recheck;
   - bounded automation/control;
   - behavioral analytics;
   - replay/decision integrity.

3. **Commercial sharpness**  
   Does each section sell one useful idea rather than explain the whole product?

4. **Credibility**  
   Is the copy specific enough to feel real without becoming technical documentation?

5. **Trader language**  
   Would a normal FX/Gold/Synthetic trader understand the words without needing Zugrio vocabulary explained first?

6. **Truthfulness**  
   Are private-build status, non-custody, read-only analytics, validation and no-performance-claim boundaries preserved?

7. **Compression**  
   Where can a sentence be shorter **without deleting a differentiator**?

8. **Missing competitive value**  
   Is any major product advantage absent or buried?

9. **Repetition**  
   Are two sections selling essentially the same point?

10. **Hierarchy**  
    Are the best advantages given the strongest headlines?
