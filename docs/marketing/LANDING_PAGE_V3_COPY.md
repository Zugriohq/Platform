## 15. FAQ

### What can I use today?
**The current public experience is a product preview and waitlist. Zugrio is in development and private validation. Public trading access is not yet available.**

### Which markets are first?
**FX, Gold and Synthetic Indices are the initial product tracks. Each requires its own data, calibration, cost and validation work.**

### Does Zugrio use one trading strategy?
**No. Zugrio is designed around versioned Methods and entry models rather than one universal setup. Initial supported Methods will be released explicitly as they are implemented and validated.**

### Does Zugrio guarantee profitable trades?
**No. Trading involves risk of loss. Zugrio does not guarantee a return, win rate or profitable outcome.**

### What happens when a setup was valid but price moves?
**The original decision remains in the record, while current entry economics and other current conditions are re-evaluated. A valid setup does not automatically mean the currently available entry still qualifies.**

### How does automation work?
**Signal, Semi-Auto, Auto and Full Auto represent different levels of delegated authority. Execution remains bound to account, Method, market and risk rules. Full Auto remains gated until its broader portfolio requirements are validated.**

### Which brokers are planned?
**cTrader and MT5 are initial integration directions. Broader broker/venue support is designed to sit behind adapter contracts and will be announced by actual released capability.**

### What happens after I join?
**You’ll receive product updates and early-access invitations as access opens. Joining the waitlist does not create a trading account, connect a broker or authorise trading.**

## 18. Copy rules

### Use
- evaluates
- detects
- compares
- records
- preserves
- flags
- blocks
- permits under mandate
- reconciles

### Avoid
- knows
- believes
- thinks
- remembers
- predicts with certainty
- guarantees
- beats the market
- institutional-grade without named standard
- “AI-powered” as the primary value proposition

### Tone
- concise
- technically grounded
- trader-relatable
- calm
- confident without certainty theatre
- sophisticated without architecture jargon

### Glance vs audit language
Public copy uses familiar language:
- Current conditions
- Authority
- Decision history
- Availability / Coverage

Internal terms such as Moment, Mandate, Model Applicability or Capability Scope may appear in deeper technical/audit contexts, not as mandatory public labels.
