# Zugrio Hero Copy Decision — 24 September 2026

Status: **FROZEN FOR LANDING APPLICATION**

## Decision

**Headline**

> **A good setup can become a bad entry.**

**Subheadline**

> **Zugrio finds setups that fit your strategy, evaluates each one in the market that produced it, and keeps rechecking what still qualifies — within your limits, with every decision on record.**

## Why this is the hero

This line is the strongest public expression of Zugrio's architecture because it turns the master thesis — **The chart is not the market** — into a trading truth the user has felt.

It also passes the enemy test better than the previous acquisition headlines:

- **Find your setup. Know what's next.** is clear but generic enough for many scanners/alert products.
- **See the setup before it becomes a signal.** is strong and specific, but primarily owns opportunity progression rather than the whole Zugrio proposition.
- **A good setup can become a bad entry.** exposes the problem Zugrio is architected to solve after detection: the signal can age, market conditions can change, entry economics can deteriorate, account state can change, and action still remains bounded by the user's control.

The line does not promise performance. It states a market/trading reality.

## Why the subheadline is architecturally complete

The sentence carries the five underlying jobs without requiring the user to learn the internal Five-M vocabulary:

- **finds setups that fit your strategy** → Method / Strategy
- **in the market that produced it** → Market
- **keeps rechecking what still qualifies** → Moment / current conditions
- **within your limits** → Mandate / Control
- **with every decision on record** → Memory / decision history

The phrase **"keeps rechecking what still qualifies"** is deliberate. It is preferred to **"rechecks it until you act"** because Zugrio supports Signal, Semi-Auto, Auto and Full Auto. The architecture must not imply that every valid path ends in a manual click by the trader.

## Consequences for the page

1. **See the setup before it becomes a signal.** returns to the Opportunity Progression section beside FORMING → READY → TRIGGERED, where it is strongest and visually proven.

2. The old Current Conditions headline **A good setup can become a bad entry.** no longer repeats the hero. Its application copy becomes:
   > **What qualified then may not qualify now.**

3. The separate **After a signal fires / The signal fired. The market kept moving.** section is merged into Current Conditions rather than remaining a second prose section selling the same idea.

4. **The signal fired. The market kept moving.** remains as a supporting proof line under Current Conditions.

5. **The chart is not the market.** remains the master company thesis and closing brand signature. It is not displaced by the acquisition hero.

## Freeze rule

Do not reopen the hero because another writer prefers a different sentence.

Reopen only if one of these occurs:

- material product architecture changes;
- real trader/user testing shows a comprehension problem;
- real trader/user testing shows the line is not distinctive/credible;
- legal/compliance review identifies a truthfulness problem;
- the product category changes enough that the headline no longer describes the central problem.

Copy taste alone is not evidence.
