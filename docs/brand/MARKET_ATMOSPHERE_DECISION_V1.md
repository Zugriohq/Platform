# Zugrio Market Atmosphere Decision V1

Status: selected for the Landing V4 candidate; not part of the canonical logo geometry.

## Concepts reviewed

### A — Market Topography
An abstract, depth-based market field built from layered contour surfaces and restrained silver light.

Strengths:
- directly supports “The chart is not the market” by implying a larger environment around the visible price surface;
- does not rely on a literal mountain;
- avoids the common glowing-network globe cliché;
- can animate slowly through depth drift, local bloom and a controlled grazing-light pass;
- remains compatible with the monochrome/graphite/silver identity.

### B — Global Market Field
A dark spherical field using restrained orbital/data geometry without a country map.

Strengths:
- immediately communicates global scale;
- works well in static institutional material.

Risks:
- globe/sphere territory is heavily used across fintech, trading and enterprise technology;
- more likely to resemble existing competitors or generic global-market imagery;
- less directly tied to Zugrio’s mechanism-aware product thesis.

## Landing V4 selection

**Market Topography (Concept A)** is selected for the landing candidate.

It is treated as a living atmospheric layer, not a logo, badge or permanent brand symbol.

Production motion:
- 19 s low-amplitude field drift;
- 13 s slow bloom variation;
- 12.5 s intermittent grazing-light pass;
- motion density reduced on mobile;
- continuous motion disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.

The rejected ridge/mountain SVG must not return.
