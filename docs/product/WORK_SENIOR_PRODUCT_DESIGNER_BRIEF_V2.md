# Zugrio Work Brief v2 — Expanded Product Design Research

Status: next-stage exploration after Meridian / Continuum / Parallax.  
Related issue: #7.

## Mission

Do not converge yet.

The first three concepts were useful baseline hypotheses. The founder wants the search space widened before selecting a production direction.

Your job is to research and explore what the **peak attainable Zugrio product experience** could be across the whole product, while preserving all authority and product constraints.

## Important changes from the first brief

- Do **not** assume Continuum, Meridian or Parallax is the final direction.
- Do **not** preserve the rejected periwinkle/cobalt-violet color direction.
- Do **not** limit exploration to static dashboard grids.
- Do **not** force internal five-M terminology into every visible label.
- Treat product language as part of design quality.

## Research requirement

Before producing new directions, study current interaction patterns in:
- advanced trading workspaces;
- modern fintech;
- high-density professional SaaS;
- command-driven productivity software;
- multi-window/desktop products.

Focus on:
- customization;
- saved layouts;
- contextual panels;
- command palettes;
- peek/quick-preview patterns;
- system status;
- notification systems;
- education/help integration;
- settings architecture;
- product updates;
- responsive/mobile continuity;
- multi-monitor expansion;
- long-session ergonomics.

References are evidence of patterns, not templates.

## New exploration requirement

Produce at least **four additional materially different layout/system hypotheses** beyond the original three.

At minimum include:

### Adaptive Decision Workspace
The interface changes emphasis according to task:
scan → inspect → approve/act → review.

### Modular Command Canvas
Bounded customizable/resizable modules with saved layouts and immutable safety/status anchors.

### Focus + Peek
A dominant selected object with contextual preview/peek rather than permanent multi-column chrome.

### Triage / Attention Inbox
A workflow organized around what needs attention now, opening into a full Decision Case.

You may add stronger alternatives discovered through research.

## Breakthrough question

Investigate whether Zugrio should have **one static workspace** at all.

A promising hypothesis is that the modern product should preserve stable anchors while the work surface adapts to:
- discovery;
- focused decision;
- execution/approval;
- monitoring;
- review.

Test this rigorously. Do not accept it because it sounds modern.

## Modernity criteria

Every direction must be critiqued against:

- immediate orientation;
- low visual negotiation cost;
- spatial continuity;
- contextual intelligence;
- expert acceleration;
- controlled personalization;
- state transparency;
- quiet confidence;
- microcraft;
- performance perception;
- expansion capacity.

## Language requirement

For every major screen, review visible terminology.

Separate:
- internal architecture terminology;
- trader-facing terminology;
- audit/advanced terminology.

Examples to test:
- Moment → Current conditions / What's changed
- Mandate → Authority / Automation permissions
- Memory → Decision history / Case history
- Capability Scope → Availability / Coverage
- Decision Case → Decision / Trade case / Case

Do not change terminology casually; propose and compare alternatives with reasoning.

## Color exploration

Brand color is open.

Show at least four palette strategies on the **same critical product screens** so color can be judged in context.

Semantic colors remain independent.

## Required full-product IA exercise

Before visual concepts, create:
- durable product domains;
- primary navigation;
- secondary navigation;
- command/search destinations;
- contextual surfaces;
- low-frequency management areas;
- mobile mapping;
- future expansion slots.

Show how Education, What's New, Settings, Profile and History expand without turning navigation into a list of 20 items.

## Output

Deliver:

1. Research synthesis.
2. Full-product information architecture.
3. Language/terminology proposal.
4. Four or more new design-system/layout directions.
5. Comparison against Meridian / Continuum / Parallax.
6. Palette exploration.
7. Recommended hypotheses to prototype further — not a final winner unless evidence is overwhelming.
8. Adversarial review.
9. Explicit list of what remains unresolved.

## Quality bar

The design should not merely look current.

It should feel like a product that could still absorb major Zugrio expansion in five years without a navigation or interaction-system rewrite.

Aim for a system whose modernity comes from behavior and product architecture before styling.
