What’s new — Zugrio 1.0 Research
Fail-closed calibration. A status label cannot unlock signals; the exact build, strategy, family, sample, costs and confidence-bound artifact must all match.
Retest is now a route, not a law. Fresh, objectively strong BOS continuation can enter a BREAK_AND_GO research path while pullback and classic-retest routes remain measured in parallel.
One volatility language. APA and CORE now use the same Wilder/RMA ATR estimator; break/displacement strength is recorded in normalized ATR units.
Entry-route ledger. Break-and-go, shallow/medium/deep pullbacks and classic retest are tracked with fill delay, MAE, MFE and time-to-excursion without influencing the live decision path.
Specialist families are active. Forex/gold, Volatility, Boom/Crash, Jump, Step, Range Break, Drift Switch, DEX and Daily Reset use named family-specific qualification and risk scaling.
Causal data gates. Closed candles, multi-timeframe freshness, continuity, exchange-time ticks and actual event-price geometry are rechecked at capture time.
Honest outcome accounting. Gaps, ambiguous bars, reloads and missing fees cannot silently become clean wins, fixed −1R losses or zero-return observations.
APA is non-discretionary. Eligible candidates are captured automatically before outcomes; manual TAKE/PASS cherry-picking is removed.
One-launch cTrader DEMO automation. Qualified verified real and synthetic symbols can open protected demo orders, take 50% at 1R or 1.5R, move to break-even, trail broker-side, and close on a confirmed opposite structural retest. Live capital is compiled out.

Research branch for entry-policy, mathematical consistency, causal lifecycle and execution-economics validation. Live capital is unavailable.

Runtime integrity controls

CausalityStateful features use closed candles only; FIRE is revalidated at the observed event price.
Data gateM5/M15/H1/H4 plus exchange-time tick freshness and continuity are execution-critical. M1 and D1 are advisory and cannot silently starve a valid setup.
GeometryFrozen thesis, structural stop, nearest TP1, minimum 1R runway after known-cost estimate.
Outcome integrityContinuous tick first-touch; observation gaps, reloads and ambiguous OHLC paths are censored.

Calibration permission

DefaultRESEARCH ONLY

Release trust gateNo trusted calibration digest is embedded in v5.16.0. A new versioned release is required after independent validation; runtime field edits cannot unlock live capital.

Performance gateNet expectancy and its lower 95% bound must both be positive. A status string or token alone cannot unlock it.

cTrader DEMO execution boundary

Capital scopecTrader DEMO only. Live mode and live accounts are rejected in both the interface and local service.
Independent validationEvery intent is version-, age-, family-, data-, geometry-, risk- and broker-quote checked again outside the browser engine.
Sizing0.25% default; 0.50% hard per-trade ceiling, 1.50% aggregate ceiling, broker volume rounded down. Expected margin may not exceed 25% of equity and broker maximum exposure is checked before submission. Existing external positions or pending orders block entry.
Provider scopeThis release is bound to Deriv cTrader DEMO. A future broker or prop-firm adapter must declare its own provider identity, symbol mapping, order semantics and acceptance tests.
ProtectionA hard broker SL/TP accompanies the market order and is reconciled. An unconfirmed or weakened stop/target causes fail-closed liquidation and disarming.
Profit managementClose 50% at configurable 1.0R or 1.5R; move the remainder to break-even only after that close is confirmed; activate cTrader server-side trailing at 1.5R with a 0.75R distance.
Reversal defenceThe hard SL remains authoritative. A fresh, opposite, family-qualified structural retest can request a risk-reducing close; after favorable progress, partial profit, break-even and broker trailing continue mechanically.
Uncertain responseReserve-before-send and reconciliation prevent blind duplicate entries or partial closes after a timeout/restart.
