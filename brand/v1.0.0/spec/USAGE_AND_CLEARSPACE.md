# Zugrio V1 Usage and Clear Space

## Primary wordmark

Use flat silver or white on dark operational surfaces.  
Use black on light surfaces.  
Use the signature metallic treatment only for major brand moments: initialization, key presentation covers and controlled campaign art.

## Monogram

The Z monogram is a supporting identifier only. It does not replace the full wordmark where the brand name can reasonably be shown.

## Clear space

Preserve at least **0.35x of the wordmark cap height** around the mark.

Do not crowd the wordmark with:
- UI labels;
- card edges;
- badges;
- container borders;
- unrelated decorative marks.

## Minimum digital size

- wordmark: **96 px** wide;
- monogram: **18 px** wide.

## Prohibited

- font substitution;
- independent letter redrawing;
- altered G incision;
- altered R leg;
- altered O contour;
- stretched or condensed geometry;
- random glow or gradient treatments;
- generic chrome-template treatment.
